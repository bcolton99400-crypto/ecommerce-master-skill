# Compliance Rules

## Universal baseline
- Avoid absolute/superlative claims such as 最、第一、唯一、顶级、极致、完美、彻底、100% unless legally and evidentially supportable in the user's supplied material.
- Every numeric performance claim, certification, ranking, official status, award, test result or comparative claim needs a reliable source.
- Do not fabricate reports, approval numbers, patents, sales, reviews, endorsements or certifications.
- Do not directly disparage competitors. Prefer factual self-description and objective comparisons.

## Risk routing
- Medical/health/regulated products: never invent efficacy or treatment claims; rely only on supplied authorization and evidence.
- Ordinary food: do not imply prevention, treatment, health improvement or disease-specific benefit. Keep to ingredients, taste, nutrition facts when supported, packaging, process and usage scene.
- Fitness/body-management products: avoid diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation and guaranteed-effect language unless appropriately authorized and supported.
- Cosmetics/beauty: do not turn visual appearance into unsupported guaranteed efficacy.

## Review
For every risky claim record:
- claim
- source/evidence
- safe wording
- risk reason

If evidence is absent, soften to a visible fact or remove the claim.

This is a content-safety workflow, not a substitute for legal counsel or platform pre-review.
