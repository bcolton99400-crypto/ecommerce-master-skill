# Production Loop

## Before generation
Confirm the unified storyboard and identify the product-truth constraints for every image.

## Prompt checklist
- Product identity and exact structure
- Viewpoint / perspective
- Composition and focal hierarchy
- Camera and lens language
- Lighting direction and softness
- Materials and finishes
- Environment and styling
- Human model/action if relevant
- Text hierarchy and placement
- Aspect ratio and resolution
- Negative constraints

## After generation
Check:
1. Product fidelity
2. Structural accuracy
3. Perspective
4. Materials and color
5. Human anatomy/action when present
6. Text spelling and layout
7. Claim safety
8. Cross-image visual consistency

If a defect changes product truth, fix it before polishing aesthetics.
