# Furniture Product Lock

Use this reference whenever the product is furniture or a structured home product.

## Must remain unchanged
- Overall silhouette
- Width/height/depth proportions
- Number and position of doors, drawers, shelves, and grids
- Glass-door/opening configuration
- Leg/base construction
- Hardware and handles
- Material and finish
- Product color
- Distinctive structural details

## Camera and perspective
If a product is placed into another scene, preserve the requested scene camera angle and make the product conform to that perspective. Do not independently rotate, widen, narrow, stretch, or redesign the product to make the composition easier.

## Material controls
Describe and preserve materials explicitly: matte paint, high-gloss lacquer, wood veneer, glass, stainless steel, PU, acrylic, stone, metal, etc. Never substitute a visually similar material without instruction.

## Scene edits
Background, floor, wall, props, bedding, plants, appliances, and people may be edited when requested. These edits must not change the product's geometry or finish.

## QA
Compare generated output against the source product image for silhouette, proportions, openings, legs, materials, color, and perspective before delivery.
